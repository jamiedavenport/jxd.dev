[Gotowy](https://gotowy.jxd.dev) is my AI-first, minimalist task manager designed to replace Todoist. In this article I'll share the product and technical details around the build.

# Features

Gotowy was designed to be simple, fast and affordable. As I wrote in my [Thoughts on Software](/writing/thoughts-on-software) article, I believe that software should be both accessible and single-purpose.

AI is making it easier than ever to build incredibly simple interfaces because most of the work can happen in the background. This is in stark contrast to the software of the past where complex and tedious forms dominated.

With that in mind these are the features I decided to focus on.
