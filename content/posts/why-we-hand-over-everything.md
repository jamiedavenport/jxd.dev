Most consultancies are structured around retention. The code lives in their accounts, the infrastructure runs on their subscriptions, and the knowledge lives in their heads. None of this is an accident. It makes the next invoice easier to send.

We do the opposite. Every JXD engagement ends with a full handover: the repositories, the infrastructure, the documentation, and the context. If we were hit by a bus the day after handover, your team could keep shipping without us.

## What a full handover actually means

Handover is not a zip file and a goodbye call. It means the code is in your organisation's accounts from day one. It means CI/CD, monitoring, and infrastructure are configured under your ownership, not proxied through ours. It means written documentation that a new engineer could onboard from, and a working session where we walk your team through the decisions, not just the artefacts.

It also means the boring parts: environment variables inventoried, third-party services listed with billing owners, runbooks for the things that page you at 2am.

## Why we would give up the leverage

Because the leverage is the problem. A consultancy that needs lock-in to keep clients is admitting the work does not speak for itself. We would rather be re-hired because the last engagement was excellent than because leaving is painful.

There is a selfish reason too. Owning nothing keeps us honest about quality. Code we hand over gets read by other engineers, so it has to hold up. That pressure is good for the work.
