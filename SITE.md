# jxd.dev — Site structure

The site is the only conversion channel. Every page sells; no page is filler.

## Pages

1. **Home** — the README translated to web. Who it's for, "weeks, not quarters", the three engagement modes (Build / Rescue / Embed), values as a scannable strip, one clear call to action. Must work standalone — most visitors see nothing else.

2. **Work** — proof. Case studies as problem → what we did → outcome. Anonymised until products/clients are ready to be named; Margin anchors it when it is.

3. **How we work** — practices expanded: continuous shipping, weekly demos and notes, production-grade from day one, direct access, full handover. Consider publishing a sample weekly note — transparency made tangible.

4. **Products** — the full-stack template, PM suite, and PolicyStack as evidence of craft, framed as "the tooling behind our speed". Each gets its own page once named.

5. **Blog** — proof through writing. Three lanes:
   - *Build logs* — real posts from building the products; doubles as Work content.
   - *AI-native practice* — how we ship in weeks without sacrificing rigour; what ideal clients are searching for.
   - *Opinionated craft* — short takes that express the values ("why we hand over everything").

   Staleness guards: no prominent dates, no promised cadence, launch with 3–4 posts minimum.

6. **About** — the founder. Solo is a feature: clients get the engineer, not a rotating bench. Who, 10+ years, why JXD, London.

7. **Contact** — plain email or short form, plus one sentence on what happens next.

## Deliberately excluded

- Service-by-service SEO pages — dilutes the boutique positioning.
- Pricing page — engagement-shaped work doesn't price on a page; the "honesty on scope" value carries the trust concern.

## Positioning notes (from competitor research, July 2026)

- The combination "craft-led + AI-native + solo-senior + own tooling" is an unoccupied corner: AI-native shops (360 Labs) don't lead with craft; craft shops (thoughtbot, YLD) are larger and slower; no-lock-in marketers (Selleo) are volume shops.
- Every credible comparable leans on named proof (clients, retention stats, open source). Naming Margin and the products is the biggest available upgrade to the site.
- thoughtbot's precedent: open-sourcing their template (Suspenders) and publishing their Playbook became their best marketing. The same play is open for the JXD template and How-we-work page.

## Minimum viable cut

Home + Work + About, with practices and products folded into Home.
